WHY ACADEMIC
INTEGRITY MATTERS?

This website help students understand what honest academic work looks like in the age of AI. It explains plagiarism, how to cite your sources, how copyright and fair use work, and how to use AI tools without crossing the line. It was built for students who want to learn the right way, so everything here is free to read and needs no account or personal information.
