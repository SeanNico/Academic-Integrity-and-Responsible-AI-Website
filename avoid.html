AVOID PLAGIARISM

how to avoid plagiarism.

Cite your
Sources

Always acknowledge the original author whenever you use their ideas or information.

Use Quotation
Marks

Put quotation marks around exact words taken from another source and provide a citation.

Paraphrase
Properly

Express information using your own understanding and sentence structure while still crediting the original source.

Keep Track of
References

Record the websites, books, articles, and other materials you use during research.

Check Your
Work

Test your knowledge on academic integrity and see how well you understand responsible AI use.Review your assignments to make sure all borrowed ideas and information are properly acknowledged.
