CITATION

Learn how to properly credit sources and avoid plagiarism.

What is Citation?

Citation is the practice of acknowledging the sources of information you used in your work. It gives credit to the original authors and allows readers to find the sources themselves.

What is Referencing

Referencing is the list of sources you used at the end of your work. It provides full details of the citations so readers can find the original sources.

Importance of Referencing

Referencing is important because it gives credit to the original authors, helps avoid plagiarism, and allows readers to verify your sources.

Tips and Guidelines for Proper Referencing

Always keep track of your sources as you research. Use the citation style required by your instructor, and double-check your citations for accuracy.

Common referencing format

Common formats include APA, MLA, and Chicago. Each has specific rules for formatting citations and references.

Key takeaway

Always cite your sources properly to maintain academic integrity. Referencing is a sign of respect for the work of others.

UNDERSTANDING OF CITATION

What is Citation?

Citation is the process of giving credit to the original author or source whenever you use someone else's words, ideas, information, or research in your work. It helps readers identify where the information came from and allows them to verify the sources.

REFERENCING

What is Referencing?

Referencing is a process of listing all the sources of information used in an academic paper, research, or school project. These sources may include books, website, journals, articles, and other materials. It gives proper credits to the original authors and shows where the information came from.

Importance of Referencing

Referencing is important because it helps students avoid plagiarism and shows honesty in their academic work. It also makes research more reliable because readers can check the original sources. Through referencing, students show respect for the ideas and efforts of other authors.

Common Referencing Formats

APA: Commonly used in education, social sciences, and research. MLA: Commonly used in literature, language, and humanities. Chicago: Often used in history and other academic subjects.

TIPS AND GUIDELINES FOR PROPER REFERENCING

1. Always list the sources you used in your research.

2. Use the correct referencing style required by your teacher.

3. Include the author's name and publication date when available.

4. Arrange your references alphabetically by the author's last name in APA style.

5. Include the title of the article, book, or website.

6. Add the URL when using online sources.

7. Make sure your references match the citations in your paper.

8. Never create fake sources or invent authors.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Referencing is not just about listing websites or books. It is about giving credit to the original authors, avoiding plagiarism, and showing honesty in academic work.
