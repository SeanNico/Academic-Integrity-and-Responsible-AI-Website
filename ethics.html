Professional Ethics

Learn what plagiarism is, why it is a problem

Honesty & Integrity

Always provide truthful and original work. Do not cheat, plagiarize, or misrepresent information.

Proper CItation & Referencing

Acknowledge the original authors and sources of information used in your work.

Respect for Intellectual Property

Respect the rights of creators by not copying, distributing, or using their work without proper permission or acknowledgment.

Accountability

Take responsibility for your actions, decisions, and the content you submit, including AI-assisted work.

Fairness & Respect

Treat others equally, respect different perspectives, and avoid using AI or technology to discriminate or harm others.

Responsible AI Use

Use AI as a support tool for learning and productivity, not as a replacement for your own understanding, critical thinking, and effort.

DOING THE RIGHT THING

What is Professional Ethics

Professional ethics is about doing the right thing and being responsible for our actions. In this website, we applied ethical principles by providing accurate information, giving proper credit to sources, respecting intellectual property, and promoting the responsible use of AI. We believe that honesty, accountability, fairness, and respect are important in maintaining academic integrity and building trust in the digital world.
