Purpose & Importance

Learn Purpose and Importance of Copyright.

Purpose

Intellectual property guarantees that creators are fairly compensated for their innovations. This also promotes innovation, enabling creators to see their work come to fruition. It diminishes consumer confusion, copying, and unauthorized use.

Importance

Intellectual Property grants protection and rights that benefit creators, and it encourages people to develop their own creative ideas, creating opportunities and opening the door to new businesses through which they can establish their identity and maintain their reputation.
