DIRECT

common types of plagiarism

Definition

Changing a few words or rearranging sentences from an original source without giving credit to the original author

Example

Rewriting a sentence from an article using different words but not citing its source.

Reminder

Not everything you find online is free to use without acknowledgment. Even if you change the wording, the original idea still belongs to its creator. Remember to give credit where credit is due. Academic integrity starts with honesty and respect for other people's work.
