PLAGIARISM

Learn what plagiarism is, why it is a problem

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit to the original creator. It can happen intentionally or unintentionally, especially when students are unfamiliar with proper citation and referencing.

Why is Plagiarism a Problem?

Plagiarism affects academic honesty and the value of a student's work. It prevents students from developing their own ideas and can lead to academic consequences. Understanding plagiarism helps students become responsible learners who respect the work and creativity of others.

Reminder

Not everything you find online is free to use without acknowledgment. Even if you change the wording, the original idea still belongs to its creator. Remember to give credit where credit is due. Academic integrity starts with honesty and respect for other people's work.
